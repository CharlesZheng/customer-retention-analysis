# Customer Retention Performance Review

An interactive Business Analyst portfolio project exploring why revenue growth
slowed for a fictional e-commerce company despite continued customer
acquisition.

## Live dashboard

Open `index.html` locally or publish the repository with GitHub Pages.

## Business question

Why did revenue growth slow in Q4, and which actions should the business
prioritize to improve customer retention?

## What the project includes

- Interactive filters for region, acquisition channel, and customer segment
- KPI definitions for revenue, orders, repeat purchase rate, and delivery time
- Monthly revenue and retention trend analysis
- Customer support ticket driver analysis
- Prioritized recommendations with owners and target timelines
- Business requirements and analysis summary documents
- A small CSV extract that documents the headline monthly dataset

## Key finding

The analysis identifies delivery performance as a plausible root cause of
retention pressure. Average delivery time increases in Q4 while repeat purchase
rate declines and shipping-related support tickets increase.

## Recommendations

1. Create a weekly fulfillment SLA dashboard by region and carrier.
2. Trigger service-recovery outreach for customers affected by late delivery.
3. Track 60-day repeat purchase rate by acquisition channel before increasing
   paid campaign budgets.

## Notes

This project uses simulated data and a fictional company. It is intended to
demonstrate business analysis, KPI design, root-cause analysis, dashboard
communication, and recommendation prioritization.
