# Executive Analysis Summary

## Situation

Northstar Commerce continued to acquire customers throughout the year, but
revenue momentum weakened in Q4. The dashboard reviews commercial performance
alongside customer-experience indicators to identify a plausible driver.

## Findings

1. Revenue growth slows after September rather than continuing its earlier
   trajectory.
2. Repeat purchase rate declines during the same Q4 period.
3. Average delivery time rises materially in Q4.
4. Late-delivery and order-tracking issues become the largest support-ticket
   drivers.
5. Returning customers remain the most important segment for sustainable
   revenue, making retention improvement a high-priority opportunity.

## Interpretation

The trends suggest that delivery reliability is weakening customer experience
and suppressing repeat purchasing. This is an analytical hypothesis, not proof
of causation. The next operational step is to validate the relationship using
order-level fulfillment data and customer cohorts.

## Recommended actions

| Priority | Action | Owner | Timeline | Success measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Create a weekly fulfillment SLA dashboard by carrier and region | Operations | 30 days | Fewer orders delivered after five days |
| 2 | Launch a service-recovery workflow for customers affected by late delivery | CRM Marketing | 45 days | Higher 60-day repeat rate for contacted customers |
| 3 | Compare 60-day repeat rate by acquisition channel | Growth Analytics | 60 days | Campaign decisions incorporate retention quality |

## Next analysis

- Join order-level delivery timestamps with customer repeat behavior.
- Measure 30-day and 60-day retention by first-order experience.
- Compare carriers and fulfillment locations.
- Test whether service-recovery outreach improves repurchase behavior.
